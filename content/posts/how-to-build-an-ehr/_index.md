Electronic Health Records (EHRs) are digital versions of patients' paper charts. They contain real-time, patient-centered records that make information available instantly and securely to authorized users. EHRs can contain a patient's medical history, diagnoses, medications, treatment plans, immunization dates, allergies, radiology images, and laboratory test results.
