This is a 7 part series on a way to build an Electronic Health Records (EHR) System. EHRs are digital versions of patients’ paper charts. They contain real-time, patient-centered records that make information available instantly and securely to authorized users. EHRs can contain a patient’s medical history, diagnoses, medications, treatment plans, immunization dates, allergies, radiology images, appointments, and laboratory test results.
